Welcome to KCC

A Legacy of Faith and Education

Founded in 1927, Kabankalan Catholic College has served generations of learners in Kabankalan City. KCC forms students with Knowledge, Character, and Competence, preparing them to contribute positively to society.

Guided by the call “Teach Ye All Nations” (Matthew 28:19), the College brings together learning, Catholic values, and community service.
