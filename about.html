About Kabankalan Catholic College

A Catholic learning community serving Kabankalan City since 1927.

Our story

A tradition of Catholic education

Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. was founded in 1927 and is located on Guanzon Street, Barangay 1, Kabankalan City, Negros Occidental.

KCC is committed to providing quality and inclusive Catholic education rooted in the Gospel. Its mission is to empower members of the school community through innovative research and meaningful community engagement so they can become agents of societal transformation.

School motto: “Teach Ye All Nations” — Matthew 28:19

Vision

Builder of the Future

KCC envisions a Christ-centered learning community of accountable and socially responsible individuals who lead positive change and serve the mission of the Diocese, the nation, and the world.

Mission

Quality and Inclusive Education

KCC commits to provide quality and inclusive Catholic education, rooted in the Gospel and driven by innovative research and meaningful community engagements, empowering its members to become effective agents of societal transformation.

KCCian Graduate Attributes

Knowledge · Character · Competence

KCC aims to form graduates who combine knowledge and practical ability with integrity, responsibility, faith, and a commitment to serve.
